BuzzyBees Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

BuzzyBees is an app for discovering and organizing local events. This page explains what information it collects and how it's used.

Account information

Creating an account requires an email address, a password, a display name, and a security question with one or more answers you choose (used only to verify your identity if you need to reset your password). Your password and security answers are never stored in plain text — both are one-way hashed before being saved.

Location

If you grant location access, your device's location is used only on your device to sort events by distance and show how far away they are. Your location is never sent to or stored on our servers.

Event data

When you create an event, its title, type, location, date, and description are stored so other users can discover it. When you RSVP, join a waitlist, or interact with an event (arrival status, guest invites, post-event reactions), that activity is visible to other users of that event, the same way it would be on any shared event listing.

What we don't do

BuzzyBees has no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not sell or share your data with any third party.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account at any time from the Profile tab. This removes your account, any events you created, and your participation (RSVPs, waitlist entries, etc.) from other people's events. This action cannot be undone.

Children's privacy

BuzzyBees is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact info listed on the app's App Store page.
