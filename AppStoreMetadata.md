## Description (4000 char max)
BuzzyBees is the easiest way to find things to do nearby — and just as easy
to host your own event for others to find.

**Discover what's happening**
Browse events by type — Sports, Party, Study Group, Meeting, Outdoor — or
filter by location, date, time, and keyword. Sort by distance, popularity,
or how soon they're happening.

**RSVP with one tap**
See exactly who else is going, join a waitlist when an event is full, and
get notified the moment a spot opens up. Reminders can be scheduled for a
day, hours, or minutes before an event starts, so you never miss it.

**Host your own event**
Set a title, type, location, date, time, and description, with an optional
capacity limit and minimum age. Edit or cancel anytime.

**Built-in extras that make events feel alive**
- Swarm Mode: an event only locks in once enough people commit by a deadline
- Blind Location: the exact address stays hidden until enough people RSVP
- The Buzz: see when an event is picking up momentum in real time
- En Route: let others know you're on your way or already there
- Echoes: leave a one-word vibe check after an event wraps up
- Plus-Ones: bring a guest using monthly invite tokens

**Your data, your control**
Delete your account at any time from the Profile tab — this removes your
account and everything tied to it immediately.

## Age Rating
No objectionable content built into the app itself, but since it hosts
user-generated event descriptions with no automated moderation yet, be
conservative in the App Store Connect age rating questionnaire (the
"User Generated Content" question) — likely 12+ or 17+ depending on how
that's answered. Consider adding basic moderation before launch if aiming
for a lower rating.

## App Privacy (App Store Connect questionnaire)
Matches `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` and `privacy.html`:
- **Email Address** — collected, linked to identity, used for App Functionality (account creation/login)
- **Location (Precise)** — collected, NOT linked to identity, used for App Functionality only (on-device distance sorting; never transmitted to or stored on the server)
- No tracking, no third-party data sharing, no advertising
